About this protection

The binder and source image are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 iterations and a random salt). The password is not included in this file, saved to browser storage or sent to a server.

Anyone with the password can read and copy the material. A downloaded encrypted copy can be attacked offline. Locking clears the view; it cannot recall copies already made. Use HTTPS or localhost if your browser does not support cryptography for local files.
